Transparent by design

One pool. One record.

Every reward is recorded against its Partner Pool and becomes part of that pool's history.

The same Trees or CO₂ cannot be allocated twice. The available balance decreases with every reward, and the pool is completed when its balance reaches zero.

Planting records and completed pools stay visible, creating a clear trail from the original environmental backing to the rewards shared with Pioneers.
