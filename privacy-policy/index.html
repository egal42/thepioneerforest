THE PIONEER FOREST

Privacy Policy

How TPF handles information.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

About this policy

The Pioneer Forest (TPF) is an independent, community-driven environmental initiative within the Pi Network ecosystem, operated by egal42. This policy applies to the TPF website, associated applications, direct Pi contributions, environmental records and Partner Pools. Privacy enquiries: egal42.pi@gmail.com.

Information handled

Depending on the activity, TPF may handle authenticated Pi usernames; payment IDs, transaction IDs, wallet addresses, amounts, memos and statuses; contribution and allocation records; voluntary messages; and limited security and technical information. Pi sign-in does not ordinarily provide TPF with Pi KYC identity documents. TPF never requests wallet passphrases or private keys.

Purposes and legal bases

Information is used to operate services, authenticate users, verify transactions, maintain environmental and participation records, answer enquiries, prevent abuse and comply with applicable obligations. Depending on the processing, the legal basis may be performance of an agreement (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), legitimate interests in secure and reliable operation (Art. 6(1)(f)), legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)), or consent for optional uses (Art. 6(1)(a)).

In-app payments and direct transfers

For payments made through the TPF Pi app, payment records can be associated with the authenticated Pi username. The Developer Portal app currently uses Pi Testnet; separate direct wallet contributions already occur on Mainnet. A direct blockchain transfer does not itself establish a verified Pi username. TPF does not automatically publish a username together with a wallet address.

Transparency and public records

TPF publishes environmental records and verification links, which may include transaction references, certificates and voluntarily provided attribution. Blockchain transactions are publicly visible independently of TPF. Public blockchain records cannot normally be erased by TPF. Public attribution or account-to-wallet disclosure beyond what is necessary for the service should be optional and clearly explained.

Service providers and transfers

Relevant third parties may include Pi Network, website and application hosting providers, blockchain infrastructure and environmental providers such as Tree-Nation. Their own terms and privacy practices may apply. Processing outside the European Economic Area may occur; applicable transfer safeguards must be assessed for the actual providers used.

Storage and retention

TPF aims to minimize production logs and retain information only for as long as necessary for its purpose, including applicable transaction and recordkeeping obligations. Temporary technical logs should be kept shorter than records required for payment verification or legal compliance. Browser storage may be used for session information and local activity history. Consent will be requested for non-essential cookies or similar technologies where required.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Rights may be limited by applicable law and immutable blockchain records. You may complain to a competent data protection authority. Contact egal42.pi@gmail.com.

Updates

This policy may be updated as TPF services develop. The latest version will appear on this page.
