THE PIONEER FOREST

Participation

Ways to support, explore and take part in The Pioneer Forest.

The Pioneer Forest is a community-driven initiative within the Pi Network ecosystem.

You can contribute Pi, explore an edition, follow the planting records, or help more people discover TPF.

This page gathers those paths in one place.

Important: Use Pi Browser for Pi wallet transactions and Pi marketplace services. Public pages and the Partner Pool workspace also work in a normal browser.

Shared Participation

Some TPF digital editions combine art with documented planting or environmental backing.

Buying an edition does not give ownership of trees or land and is not an investment. Check its product page for the artwork, backing and records included.

Ways to Participate

Direct Impact

Contribute Pi

Send a Pi contribution to the official TPF wallet to support planting. Follow the instructions so it can be recorded correctly.

How to Support →

Shared Artifacts

Take part through digital editions

Some editions or shared products connected to The Pioneer Forest may be made available through external marketplace partners.

These items record shared participation in documented impact, rather than ownership of trees, land, or environmental assets.

Public Record

Follow and engage on Fireside

Follow the Fireside channel for planting records, milestones and project updates.

Fires and replies help more people discover the forest and its records.

Visit the Fireside channel →

Forest Presence

Explore branch pages and linked records

Some forms of participation are expressed through branch pages within The Pioneer Forest, such as Elowen and earlier chapters like Guardians of Growth.

These pages hold the forest’s art and history.

Enter the Forest →

Marketplace Use

When shared products or editions are offered, they may be distributed through independent marketplace partners.

These platforms provide their own listing, purchase, and account systems. TPF provides its own product descriptions and the records supporting their stated backing.

Use Pi Browser when a Pi marketplace requires it.

What Participation Is Not

  • Not ownership of trees or land
  • Not a financial investment
  • Not a promise of future returns
