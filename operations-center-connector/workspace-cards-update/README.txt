TPF Partner Workspace Cards update

Close Operations Center, extract the ZIP, run INSTALL.bat and choose your existing TPF_ADMIN folder. Restart afterwards. The installer checks the current templates/scripts and backs them up. No app.py, settings, data, payments or pools are changed.

Updates the read-only workspace preview and Operations Center activity cards. The corresponding online workspace change is a separate website deployment. Existing and future partners use the same shared renderer and their existing colours.

Offers & orders replaces the misleading fixed heading. Completed orders show the chosen pool and readable totals; original options and payment reference remain in expandable details. Unpaid orders retain payment instructions and offer selection remains available only when needed.

Top notices follow each request. New-pool notices disappear after the first reward from that pool, or after a partner dismisses them in their browser. Dismissal is browser-local, keyed by partner, request and pool. A different browser may show the notice again until the first reward. Read-only Admin viewing does not dismiss partner notices.

Audit details in Operations Center are expandable. Required payment, planting and connection confirmations remain.
